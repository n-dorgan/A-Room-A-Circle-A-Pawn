A deck of cards with odd symbols, only [[The Fortune Teller]] seems to understand the meanings they carry. Twice now she has read your fate, and twice you have left more confused than ever before. Control of the cards belongs to [[Zephyr]] in this moment of time, but know that control of the Deck belongs to [[The Room/The Pawns/index|The Pawns]], and it is your will alone that grants its power.
