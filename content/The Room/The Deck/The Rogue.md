Along with a [[Crimson Cloak|cloak]], this card was gifted to [[Inbad]], a testament to a man on the run. Leaving behind his [[The Kingdom|kingdom]], he did what he must to save the dying star, and would do it again if it meant dying himself.

It was later seen again in passing, stealing [[The Gem]], only to leave it behind in [[Ruin]] as he returns to his post, guarding [[The Throne]] with [[The Knight]].
