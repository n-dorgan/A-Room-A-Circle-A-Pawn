Adjacent to the docks where [[The Luna]] arrived from the sea beyond, the boathouse used to be a core part of Lubellum's identity. Remnants of its former majesty can be found through the pictures on the wall, and the empty space surrounding the building containing the faded markings of a towering structure that once stood by it. Now, all that stands is a small, seemingly uninhabited residence with a faint light emanating from the stunted beacon above it.

After the first night in Lubellum, a return trip to the boathouse revealed a drastically shifted landscape. Perhaps the most torn place in the town, the boathouse exhibited a monochromatic gradient, bleached into a black and white pallor that stripped any remaining character from the already dilapidated shack. The damage that [[The Shadows]] caused appears to be irreversible, permanently marking the boathouse, and the rest of Lubellum, with the chaos of the night. One can only hope that it does not spread any further...
