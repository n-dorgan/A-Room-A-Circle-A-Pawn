The party arrived at this strange town from pathways that seemed to disappear as soon as they stepped foot on the sacred land beneath them. As they got their bearings, they were faced with a blight consuming their first night, enveloping the town in shadows that were only repelled by the light of the Beginning. A strange thought fell upon them the next day, when they realized that no more than 6 people inhabited the town, with many seeming to appear and disappear at a moment's notice. As they rushed to escape the uncanny sight, the tendrils of shadow chased them to the Waypoint, where they witnessed Anthro's apparent untimely demise. The fate and true location of Lubellum may very well be forever left unknown...
