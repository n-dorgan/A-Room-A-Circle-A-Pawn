A half-finished -- or perhaps half-destroyed -- temple sits atop the east cliff of Lubellum, presumably looking towards the sunrise, if such a thing even were to occur in this realm. The scaffolding of an impressive cathedral adorns the temple, with stained glass windows depicting histories of the gods before, and the gods before those as well. The gaps in the ceiling created an atmosphere open to the elements, with a beam of moonlight illuminating a stone altar towards the back of the temple.

[[The Informant]] claims that this temple was once dedicated to him, implying that he is the Forgotten God from long ago. There is some truth in those words: the temple was clearly once a fount of vitality for Lubellum, now reduced to a memory soon to be forgotten along with its god. But what is a god? Who else might find Origins in Lubellum?
