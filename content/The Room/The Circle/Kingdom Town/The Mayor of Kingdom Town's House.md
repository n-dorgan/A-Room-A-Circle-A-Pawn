
**Current Occupants:** [[Mayor of Kingdom Town]], the mayor's partner

This structure is a glass house designed to be the primary place of residence for the Mayor of Kingdom Town. The house is a marker of status, but also a sign of trust, with its clear walls and open, transparent design acting as a bridge of respect between the mayor and his community. Possession of the house rotates with the current mayor, and intends to place the mayor in the center of their people, always available for whatever their constituents may need.

Though the house is open, it is protected by a series of barriers, to ensure the family's safety, even with such little privacy. An invisible barrier envelopes the entire structure; any objects attempting to pass through are faced with an elastic recoil, returning to strike any object or person who would be foolish enough to throw stones at a glass house. However, vibrating an object at the same frequency as the barrier has the capacity to create a resonance effect, collapsing the shield and revealing an unfortunate design flaw. As an additional security measure, passing through the entryway without disarming the security system will trigger a fine, aromatic mist sedating those in its presence.

The party, while guests in Kingdom Town, successfully attempted to break into the house, where they found a quaint living room, with various spaces for lounging, eating, and discussing business: the same picture that one would find if viewing the house from the outside.

In the living room, photos were hung above the mantel of a fireplace, depicting the following:
- A young boy
- Two men together, seemingly the mayor and his partner
- A man and an older woman
- The same two men with a boy and a girl
- A dog, rolling in the grass, a blissful expression across its face

Additionally, medical robes were seen protruding from one of the closets in the hallway, signifying the current mayor's extensive education and involvement with the town.
