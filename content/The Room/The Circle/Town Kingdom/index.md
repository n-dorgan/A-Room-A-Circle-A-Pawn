A floating, inverted mountain towering above a  pond of [[Silver Liquid|silver]] makes this kingdom a sight to behold. Chains and ropes hang from the cliffsides, offering scaffolding to scale upwards in a treacherous climb, though it would be much easier to visit the kingdom by air.

Having the unfortunate name of "Town Kingdom," it oftentimes gets confused with its sister city, [[The Room/The Circle/Kingdom Town/index|Kingdom Town]]. The [[The Room/The Pawns/index|party]] initially intended to travel here via a [[waypoint]], though ended up at the latter by mistake. These names were chosen in a time forgotten, derivatives of an older dialect from a different world.

The kingdom's legacy is rooted in its Dwarven populace, a population able to withstand working within the mountain itself to benefit from its lush deposit of constantly renewing natural resources and the secrets to long and prosperous life. These resources were actually the spark for their diplomatic relationship with Kingdom Town, starting as a medical partnership, which has since evolved into something much greater.

Now, its Dwarven heritage is known only through its history scrolls and cultural architecture. Walking through the kingdom itself reveals an incredibly diverse population, acting as home to all peoples and the crafts and skills they bring with them. When the Moon is right, the grand Festival takes shape, transforming the terrain into a joyous revelry to the [[The Room/The Players/index|gods]], where each life is examined and valued for its intrinsic worth on this plane.
