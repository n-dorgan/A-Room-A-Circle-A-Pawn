Filled with cuisines from across the realm, this strip of stalls contains everything to satiate even the fiercest hunger, and cure even the deadliest disease. Most dishes take a unique spin on classic dishes, with every possible variety being offered. If you can't find what you're looking for here, keep walking and you'll run into it eventually! Look out for peddling merchants though - their kabobs are to die for, but you may never sleep again if you can't handle the spices mined from the mountain's reserves below.
