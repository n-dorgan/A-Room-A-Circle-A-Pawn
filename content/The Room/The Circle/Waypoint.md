The Waypoint is a useful tool for transportation. Entering the room, brilliant cosmic projections can be seen strewn against the walls, a complete view of this world as it is known. It is crucial to be specific and clear of mind when outsourcing travel to this spectral occurrence, or else fate may catch you astray from your intended destination
