One of the most reputable medical schools in this realm, its alumni have achieved great success and fame after graduating from its rigorous 10-year curriculum focusing on treating uncurable ills, by bending the Weave to sew new patches of development into medicine's history. Notable figures who have graduated from Kingdom Town's Medical School are Astrassios the Wayward, Texhia the Bringer, the [[Mayor of Kingdom Town]], and the mage which helped the party slow the progression of Æ's otherworldly ailment.
