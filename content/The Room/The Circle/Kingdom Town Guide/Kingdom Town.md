The party entered Kingdom Town after travelling through the Lubellum Waypoint and being misdirected towards the nearest "Kingdom". While in Kingdom Town, they visited an alchemist who provided them with the resources to temporarily satiate Æ's developing condition. In exchange, he asked the party to deliver a potion to his grandmother in the mountains, which was the party's intended destination regardless. Before leaving, the party invaded the Mayor's house and stopped by the medical school for supplies on the road.
