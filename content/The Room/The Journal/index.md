A mostly blank journal found by Zephyr in the [[The Fortune Teller|Fortune Teller's]] residence in [[The Room/The Circle/Lubellum/index|Lubellum]], words keep appearing at strange times. The journal has a particular affinity to [[The Workshop]], where all the text has appeared thus far, written by a faceless, nameless, and powerless [[The Author|author]]. While it might be a guide to this world, or something else entirely, the pages that form doesn't seem to have any manner of coherence to them, containing cryptic warnings and stories without real substance to what they are meant to point to.
