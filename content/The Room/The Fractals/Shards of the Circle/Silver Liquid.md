This fluid has formed an integral part of [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom's]] landscape, seen at the "base" of the mountain, on a waterfall over the edge of the [[Haven of Sight]], and in the [[Fount of Life]] that acts as a source of vitality for all.

It has a clear reflection when looked at, no matter how turbulent the flow is. Drinking it has shown a great struggle, with [[Æ|Æ]] demonstrating both the dangers and curious properties that it contains.
