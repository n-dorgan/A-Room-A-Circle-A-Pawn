Gifted after Jaime's display of determination towards protecting his values, [[The Protector]] knighted him as "Jaime, Protector of House Lancaster," offering her own scales as tribute. Upon closer inspection, it was revealed that his [[House of Lancaster|family]] crest was embossed onto the scales, apparently a natural part of their design, raising questions to their connection.
