Found in the newly constructed [[The Workshop|workshop]], the eyepiece lay hidden in a desk drawer, wrapped in a velvet cloth. Supposedly belonging to [[The Author]], it is embossed with the symbol of an ankh (☥), the eyepiece itself containing multiple lenses of different colors ready to be brought out for examination. It is a marvel of engineering, though its design and function is rather unintuitive.
