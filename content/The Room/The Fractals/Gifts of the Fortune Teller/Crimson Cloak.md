Gifted by [[The Fortune Teller]] in their first encounter, this cloak reminds you of your noble wear from your time in [[The Kingdom]]. Wearing it gives you a sense of power, restoring your status while still offering the function needed to work from the shadows. The protection it offers is immense, shielding you from even the most prescient of eyes.
