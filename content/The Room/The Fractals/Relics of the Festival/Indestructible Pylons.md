Invigorated with a blessing from [[The Protector]] while hanging from the side of the cliffs of [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom]], ordinary pylons became indestructible forces of nature. When climbing with these tools, they can slide through any surface, magically increasing your reach to always find the correct placement location to aid your ascent.
