First obtained in the [[House of Wisdom]], it was later enhanced at the forge found in the [[Domain of Might]]. Finely crafted with metals mined in the mountain, imbued with the magic of [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom]] itself, this dagger is bound to catch any foe off guard, striking true.

Retaining its original nature after being forged, both poison and phase can be used in conjunction, a vaporous mist trailing as it is drawn.

# Poison Dagger
Gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this weapon.

You may use a bonus action to will the dagger to be coated with a thick, dark gray poison. This poison lasts for one minute before dissipating, or until it makes contact with a creature.

Upon such contact, the creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw with a DC equal to 8 + Proficiency Bonus + your Dexterity, or else they take 2d10 additional poison damage and become poisoned for 1 minute. The dagger cannot be used in this way until the next dawn.

# Phase Dagger
This dagger has 3 charges. One expended charge is replenished upon dawn.

You may use a bonus action to expend a charge, willing the dagger to phase out of this plane for up to one minute. While phased, the dagger can bypass physical barriers, so that any attacks made ignore armor, shielding, and coverage. The dagger can also pass through solid objects as if they were not there.

Alternatively, you may use a bonus action to expend a charge to cause yourself to phase, weaving through [[The Room/The Shadows/index|The Shadows]]. Each time you strike with the dagger, you have a 50% chance to phase out of this plane, allowing you to travel freely as if you were ethereal.

If phased at the start of your turn, automatically return to the nearest spot within 5 ft of where you currently stand.
