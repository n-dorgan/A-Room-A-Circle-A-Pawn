An amorphous black figure, this shadow has been seen in both [[The Room/The Circle/Lubellum/index|Lubellum]] and in [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom]], in the [[Haven of Sight]], slain by the party. It encroaches from objects, elongating as the sun sets, and persisting through the night, where its power blossoms. It's purpose and intentions are unclear, but it warns of a greater threat, sometimes leaving behind a [[Black Onyx]] as a symbol of its persistence.
