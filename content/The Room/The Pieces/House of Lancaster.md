The family name that [[Jaime]] is sworn to protect, this house used to be the charge of a powerful [[The Kingdom|kingdom]]. After a brutal war, they were wiped off the face of the map, Jaime left alive as the sole survivor. He searches for their presence in this realm, hoping to find remnants of his past and his devotion. [[The Protector]] also seems to have some relation to this family, her scales sharing a history of service unknown to most.
