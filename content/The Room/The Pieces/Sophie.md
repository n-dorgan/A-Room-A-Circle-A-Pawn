Wishing nothing more than to live a simple life, she found her way to [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom]] by accident, taking a role as a bartender so she can be involved with the people of her community. She met the party once at the [[Town Kingdom Casino]] and again during the Festival in the [[Hall of Will]], where she provided some useful tips about getting along with the kingdom's culture. She wishes she had the chance to see more of this world, though lacks the means to do so.
