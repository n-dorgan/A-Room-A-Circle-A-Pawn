## Power
You have begun to remember what was once forgotten, what was once stripped from your mind. Through near death, your life has been renewed. When faced with tainted memory, you may use this ability a single time:
- You may accurately recall any event in your own history, in detail, to the extent that the timestream does not also fragment into shards.
- Alternatively, you may forge a memory, deciding yourself in any given moment what had happened in your history - perhaps you were once friends with a powerful figure you just met, or you knew a merchant from before.
The choice, like your memory, is yours to make.
