
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Jaime]]
- **Feat:** Might

## Power
You identified the core of the trial in the [[Domain of Might]], pinpointing where the anvil should be hit to enhance the forged weapon, rather than shattering it.

Once per long rest, you may use a bonus action to identify the structural weakness for any person or building, dealing double damage on your attack roll if successful. You must announce use of this feat before your attack, and if you hit your target, knowledge of the weakness lasts for your party members' use until the start of your next turn.
