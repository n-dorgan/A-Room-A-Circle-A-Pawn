
# Moonshine I
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Æ]]
- **Feat:** Tomfoolery

## Power
For stealing ale, only to dump in on the barkeep's head, you gain a +2 bonus to any rolls involving alcohol (stealing, drinking, etc.). This bonus does not apply if you have a negative modifier added to your roll from Taste of Will.

# Moonshine II
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Æ]]
- **Feat:** Audacity

## Power
You were foolish enough to waste liquid the first time around, it is only fitting that you drank it the second time. For managing to barely survive consumption of the [[silver liquid]], it formed an endoskeleton within your arm, extending downwards towards your palm.

If you are ever struck with a liquid-based attack, you may attempt to absorb it, blocking damage and emulsifying the offense in the hardened endoskeleton. On a later turn, you may expel the stored liquid as an attack action, retaining its original properties, along with potential transmutations made by its contact with the silver fluid.
