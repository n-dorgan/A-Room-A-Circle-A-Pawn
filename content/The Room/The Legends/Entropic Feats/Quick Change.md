
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Inbad]]
- **Feat:** Guile

## Power
Caught while attempting to steal, you transformed into a new person, one with status, power, and social prestige.

When someone calls your bluff, you may use your noble status to reroll any failed Deception check. You must use this new roll, even if it is worse than the original. This feature can be used a number of times per long rest up to your proficiency bonus.
