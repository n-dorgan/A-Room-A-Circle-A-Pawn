
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Zephyr]]
- **Feat:** Cunning
- "90% of gamblers stop right before they hit it big"

## Power
For your swift cunning rivalling Inbad, the party's resident rogue, you managed to deceive the high-security casino into giving you a much higher payout than deserved. You refused to stop before hitting it big.
- Once per long rest, you can push your luck to reroll any die before knowing the overall outcome.
- If you choose to do this and fail the reroll (or obtain a result equal to or worse than the original roll), gain one level of exhaustion, or worse...are the cards in your favor?
