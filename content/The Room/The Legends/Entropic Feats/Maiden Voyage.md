
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Zephyr]]
- **Feat:** Ineptitude

## Power
Talking with Sophie would have left you humiliated if you had any dignity left. But regardless, alongside embarrassment, Sophie instilled within you the wisdom of talking with women.
- Whenever you engage in cordial and respectful conversation with a woman, add +2 to all rolls.
- Choosing to act without honor will inflict a -2 penalty.
