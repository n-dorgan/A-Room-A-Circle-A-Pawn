
## Origin
- **Pawns:** [[Jaime]]
- **Feat:** Finesse
- "He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone."

## Power
You didn't throw first, but were the only able to fulfil the task at hand. For aiming true with a stone at a glass house, matching the frequency of the barrier perfectly, you demonstrated aptitude in your skills. You gain proficiency in "stones" as a weapon, and have the uncanny ability to always find a stone around you, as long as there is a glass structure nearby.
