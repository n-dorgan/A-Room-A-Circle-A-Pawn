## Power
You hold yourself with the highest honor capable of man: you pledge yourself to a kingdom you can no longer remember, that may no longer exist. Your undying devotion radiates when you speak of your cause. If you are ever in need, people will listen, because you have answered their cries before. You may soon find out how...
