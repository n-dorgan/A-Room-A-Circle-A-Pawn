Standing in the [[Domain of Might]] after completing their trial, [[The Pawns]] walk to the [[House of Wisdom]]. Before leaving, [[Zephyr]] notices the archway leading into the [[Realm of Knowledge]] has an inscription matching the previous one he saw. Just like before, the languages shifted, but the text now reads "WISDOM IS FOUND WITHIN A PERSON."

Entering Wisdom's sanctuary, the party pays closer attention to the towering statue occupying the center of the market. It faces the entryway they just came from, and sits on a circular pedestal, with its own engraving that reads "I have served my master well, now I stare into the depths of hell," in the Abyssal tongue.

Looking at the room around them, they notice a mural lining the walls of the House, circling its entire perimeter above the stalls below it. [[Inbad]] recognizes that the mural depicts the history of the [[The Room/The Circle/Dwarven Kingdom Guide/index]] - or perhaps the history of this world, or of something greater. The events travel from creation to progress to war to ruin, back to creation, all of  history contained in this one visage. A lone figure, hooded in white, adorns the mural.

Tinkering with the statue, Zephyr finds that it is able to rotate. As it rotates, the painted figure dances and travels, moving with the gaze of the statue. They represent progression through the flow of time, walking with the people as they first settled, guiding them as they built their homes, reconciling them as they recover from catastrophe. She has seen it all, and she has been with them through everything.

Not only this, but the inscription on the statue's pedestal also changes with its rotation, each taking the form of a different language. The party collectively could recognize most of the languages, but only understood the translations of some of them.
