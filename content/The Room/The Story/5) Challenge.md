Standing in the [[Domain of Might]] after completing their trial, [[The Room/The Pawns/index|the pawns]] walk to the [[House of Wisdom]]. Before leaving, [[Zephyr]] notices the archway leading into the [[Realm of Knowledge]] has an inscription matching the previous one he saw. Just like before, the languages shifted, but the text now reads "WISDOM IS FOUND WITHIN A PERSON."

# Wisdom's Watch
Entering Wisdom's sanctuary, the party pays closer attention to the towering statue occupying the center of the market. It faces the entryway they just came from, and sits on a circular pedestal, with its own engraving that reads "I have served my master well, now I stare into the depths of hell," in the Abyssal tongue.

Looking at the room around them, they notice a mural lining the walls of the House, circling its entire perimeter above the stalls below it. [[Inbad]] recognizes that the mural depicts the history of the [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom]] - or perhaps the history of this world, or of something greater. The events travel from creation to progress to war to ruin, back to creation, all of  history contained in this one visage. A lone figure, hooded in white, adorns the mural.

Tinkering with the statue, Zephyr finds that it is able to rotate. As it rotates, the painted figure dances and travels, moving with the gaze of the statue. They represent progression through the flow of time, walking with the people as they first settled, guiding them as they built their homes, reconciling them as they recover from catastrophe. She has seen it all, and she has been with them through everything.

Not only this, but the inscription on the statue's pedestal also changes with its rotation, each taking the form of a different language. The party collectively could recognize most of the languages, but only understood the translations of some of them. Starting from the entryway facing the [[Fount of Life]], and moving clockwise, the texts read as follows:

- *(???)* ???
- *(Celestial)* ?
- *(Primordial)* ?
- *(Draconic)* "Follow me to ascension beyond, so that we may protect the coming dawn"
- *(Sylvan)* "Let us build something that lasts, let us lament the time that past"
- *(Dwarfish)* "Hard I worked for the fruits of my labor, surrounded by friends, foes, and neighbor"
- *(Common)* "You know my name"
- *(Thieves' Cant)* "Wisdom within oneself is hidden, call upon that which is forbidden"
- *(Undercommon)* ?
- *(Infernal)* ?
- *(Deep Speech)* ?
- *(Abyssal)* "I have served my master well, now I stare into the depths of hell"

Unsure what to do, [[Jaime]] draws his scimitar, climbs the pedestal of the statue, and strikes its foot. Viewing from afar, [[Æ]] notices that upon doing so, an energy pulsates up through the statue, causing its eyes to glow a sharp blue.

Still unclear how to pass this trial, and ignoring the tables against the wall, Inbad uses his [[Dwarven Dagger|phase dagger]] to step through the statue's hollow armor, entering the relatively large space inside. He sees words and symbols floating through the sky, radiating a cyan color, circling over his head. When the statue is rotated along its axis, the words change form, shifting languages to match the script inscribed on its banner.

When shifted to the language of Thieves' Cant, quite familiar to Inbad, three maps were revealed to him: a glowing red map, with golden text, offering knowledge of the universe; a simple paper map, guiding the route they must follow on their quest; and an unassuming black map, with faint text, appearing to depict his [[The Kingdom|hometown]] through glyphs he does not recognize. Reaching for the last of the three, he grabs the [[Kingdom Void Map|black map]] and pockets it, causing the remaining two to disappear, now wandering among the floating text.

As Inbad makes this profound discovery, Zephyr continues to try different combinations, facing the statue back towards the Domain of Might, with the Abyssal text now vibrantly showing. Jaime, still thinking with his fists, finished his ascent up the statue, and swings his scimitar against its neck.

Inside, Inbad feels the energy start to pulsate through the statue, unnaturally drawn up from its base like a magnet, rather than the gentle pull observed before. He quickly phased out, not wanting to discover what it would do to him, when light shot through the statue's eyes in a large beam, trailing over the Anvil into the distance. It's path grazed the archway, tearing into the marble and causing the entire mountainside to tremble.

The statue looked down at Jaime, who fell ungracefully from its helmet, then looked up towards the robed figure in the mural. She walked along the painting's brushstrokes once more, finally ending up in the ruin, where she raised its arms and lowered herself into the ground.

The statue kneeled towards where she once stood, waiting for her return, for her to remember who she needs to be. With that gesture, the final torch of trials is lit, recognizing that obtaining wisdom takes time, and that they may be faced with this trial once more when they have finally comprehend its nature.

# An Incomplete Trial
A clanking sound is heard from the central foyer, a mechanical path heard opening. Before they can investigate, the statue is overcome with darkness, having the light within expelled from its body. A shadow overtakes it, allowing it to rise once more, as [[Statue's Shadow|it]] runs towards the [[Haven of Sight]].

Though they completed the trial, the pawns did not fully understand it, and so consequences must be faced. The sound of the gears turning ceased, the path not fully open, a torch unlit.

Entering the Haven, a darkness falls upon them, coming to the realization that the statue's shadow has snuffed the candle they were ordered to protect, extinguishing a peaceful era. Rushing towards the center, they find it almost peacefully standing over it, guarding the success in the party's reach.

Trying to distract the statue, Jaime swings first, allowing Inbad an opening, which he deftly uses to slip and fall to the ground. He rolls over, grasping the shadow's legs, and pulls with all his might to drag it to the floor with himself.

Meanwhile, Æ uses a previously successful strategy of summoning a series of dancing lights within the forest's foliage, illuminating the massive shadow that seems to keep growing in size as its power remains unchecked. These paltry lights are not nearly enough to weaken it, causing it to not even flinch.

And yet, as he decorates it's faceless figure with these festive bulbs, he is unable to control his magic, simultaneously summoning grease below himself, allowing Inbad's attempt to succeed, the statue slipping to fall prone, locked in a struggle with Inbad to maintain control of the other.

Zephyr attempts to retrieve the candle, sliding through the greased terrain to quickly swipe it from beneath the statue. He snatches the unlit flame, but not before the shadow sends him flying into the nearest tree, a sharp crack heard as it is felled with the force thrown at it.

Æ tries an offensive cast, failing once more, cracks forming in his magic as his eyes start to glow as if they were the dancing lights he had cast previously.

Inbad and Jaime continue to strike the figure, but the damage is not enough. Zephyr lights the candle, hoping to turn the tides of the battle by raising the sun from its rest. Though the candle is now lit, night reigns supreme, the void of the shadow entrapping any other sign of light, bringing a pitch black to all except Æ, whose lit eyes guide his sight.

With this advantage, Æ casts one more spell, striking true against the statue's shadow, while also turning himself into a [[Inspiration Sheep|sheep]], the final humiliation against a series of uncontrolled magecraft. Yet, for his determination in the process, demonstrating true power in chaotic hold of wild magic, he has gained the feat [[Unlucky]].

As the statue grows beyond control, Zephyr makes a final attempt, pulling out the strange [[The Author's Eyepiece|eyepiece]] he found earlier, placing it in front of the statues, rapidly moving through different combinations of lenses to find one that would amplify its light. As he hits just the right oval of glass, the candle's waning flame is focused to a sharp beam that pierces through the large shadow, weakening it enough for the rest of the party to finish off the job.

Only with the natural source of the forest's light could such an imposing foreign darkness be quelled. The remainders of the Statue's Shadow did not waste time as the party watched it slowly dissipate into the risen sun like the lesser [[Shadow Creature]] did before it; it seeped into the earth below, embedding itself within the soil to find protection in the darkness below. If you descend, you will certainly find this foe once more...

# The Truth Seen
With the candle now lit, the torch's flame returned, and the staircase can be heard continually expanding, the sound echoing throughout the Festival. Yet, no other sounds can be heard. As they walk towards the Haven's exit, greenery overgrowing across the arch, the party no longer hears the bustling sound of cheers from the [[Hall of Will]], nor the shouts of merchants from the House of Wisdom.

They detect a rustle of leaves behind them. Turning quickly, no one is to be found.

Until a blink.

An [[The Elder of the Forest|old man]] stands there, hunched over as he struggles to lift his head to the party. His eyes glazed over, he waits and watches.

Jaime, ever the opportunist, starts to run towards him. Before he can even place his hand on his scimitar, he finds himself knocked to the ground, the old man not appearing to have moved a muscle, except for the smile that has now formed on his lips.

"Thank you for saving my haven. This was once a peaceful place, and now you have returned it to that state. I am in your debt."

Upon general confusion from the party, Inbad was the first to ask who this strange man was. Already claiming to be the overseer of the Haven of Dexterity, he looked slightly puzzled, remarking that Inbad of all people should know who he is. He is Inbad's patron, after all, among many other things. They knew each other a long time ago in their lost Kingdom, and many a secret was shared between them.

Seeing as no one has done the party the honor of explaining anything about this realm to them, he offers them the answer to one question, as these are the limits of the information he is allowed to share currently - at least the information that he has domain over. It is only right, to repay them for their valiant assistance.

Jaime, stepping forward once more and speaking for them all, asks, "Why were we all put together?"

The man nods with a sage face, reflecting on Jaime's question and letting him know that he chose well - this is a valuable piece of information that will carry you far.

"Why you were put together...hmm...a fine question indeed. I'll tell you what the laws of this world allow you to know. You were brought here to be Pawns in a Game. Each one of you has a [[The Room/The Players/index|Player]] sponsoring you, guiding you along, using you in some cases for any purpose they deem necessary.

There are six of us, all of which you have met previously, in passing or otherwise. Or, I suppose, the better phrasing would be that you have been introduced to us all. To meet us is to know us and there is still one who was not yet represented in the trials you faced.

To move forward in this Circle of life, you must first identify who controls your will, who you depend on, and who you can trust. I am confident in your journey, and know that my teachings will guide you far."

Another blink.

Without learning his name, the man is gone, his words opening a new level of understanding on this world. The doorway into even deeper knowledge continues to open down the mountain, its sound continuing to reverb through the still Festival. Perhaps more answers can be found below, the [[Silver Liquid]] seen flowing down the sides of the staircase, bottom indistinguishable from an empty drop.

As you begin the long descent, the time to be spent unknown, the card in [[Zephyr|Zephyr's]] pocket begins to glow, transforming from [[The Comet]] into something unrevealed, its light guiding the path down.
