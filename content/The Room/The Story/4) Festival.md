
> [!abstract] Festival
> The pawns awake from the previous night and take advantage of the many luxuries the festival before them has to offer. They trek through the different realms of each room, representing core tenets of the world they inhabit, and complete the trials that they offer to them. A flame lights with each test passed, with only one unlit torch remaining.

*The wills of fate have shrouded your memories since you emerged in this realm. Their pull continues to act on your recent journeys, offering only fragments of what transpired in the festival below. Those trials which you have not remembered, you have not completed.*

The [[The pawns|party]], waking up from the night before, hear the clamoring of bells, shaken by movement in the ground below them. Looking up to the sky, a shining [[The Comet|Comet]] streaked above. Focused on the disrupting sight, the party missed the landscape of the Dwarven Kingdom shifting, walls collapsing and rising, hallways forming, and screams falling upon deaf ears as the Comet approaches. And yet, looking back down around them, they noticed people celebrating the occurrence, with fanfare already taking place by the inhabitants of the kingdom. This phenomenon is not a threat to their safety, but rather appears to be a boon to their livelihood, prompting immediate celebration across all people.

Each member stranded in a different segment of the festival, [[Inbad]] is the first to come to focus, with the bursting of laughs and cheers around him calling him to recall his [[The Kingdom|home]].

The festival represents the change that the Comet brings. The linear flow of time has no power over the landscape and the trials that the festival offers. One must understand the spatial separation of this world before all else:

# The Core

![[Fount of Life]]

*The party found their way to the center of this celebration twice during their first encounter with the trials of the festival.*

After awaking from their sleep, all (except Zephyr, who rushed to Jaime's aid) found themselves drawn to this unifying area. Walking from their respective doorways, they arrive in a circular room with a majestic, yet simple fountain in the precise center of the space. Along the walls sit five uniformly spaced arches, each leading to vastly different terrains, people, and challenges. Opposite each door hangs a simple torch, all unlit except one, found across the door towards the Haven of Sight.

Approaching the fountain, they notice that it is not water that flows from it, but the deep [[silver liquid|silver liquid]] they found leaking from the mountain before their ascent. Zephyr and Inbad both empty bottles from their bags and fill them in the running fluid, each taking 3 samples for later use, investigation, study, or ruin.

Inbad, while gathering his sample, gazes into the fountain for a moment too long. As he watches his reflection, he sees a different version of himself, from another life. He remembers why he ran, who he was, who he once loved. He knows who he is, who he will be. But it was a moment. Being pulled back to this reality, he now understands what Æ went through that first day back in [[Lubellum]]. For he too had a [[Force of Will#Taste of Will|Taste of Will]].

Æ, ever explorative and unpredictable, took a different approach to the mysterious liquid that runs through the mountain's veins. Cupping the silver into his hands, he stares as his foreign reflection, blinking back at him. He gulps the pool down, feeling normal for only a handful of seconds. It flows through every passage in his body, moving at a pace faster than the passage of time; Æ lurches over in pain, excruciating as it seeks to find an exit. He channels his magic, casting chaos bolt from his arm in a desperate attempt to fight chaos with chaos. Increasing the entropy of his own system, he expels the liquid from his body, but not before retracting control over the spell, reeling his magic back towards his soul, exerting control over the silverly thread.

It melds with his spell, turning his magic metallic, receding into his body; instead of the previous thrashing, it seeps into the open space, filling what is missing. A sharp silver color settles in his arm, as it solidifies into an endoskeleton alongside his own. Æ has gained the feat [[Moonshine#Moonshine II|Moonshine II]].
