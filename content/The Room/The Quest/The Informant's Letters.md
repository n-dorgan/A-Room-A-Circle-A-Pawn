
# [[The Room/The Circle/Lubellum/index|Lubellum]]
My dear [[Zephyr]],

Your undying allegiance to me is perhaps your most valuable quality. I would be lying if I said that your service was near complete, but it may be comforting to you to think of it as being so. For your next assignment, I am sending you somewhere...different.

It's likely you wont remember who you are, but that doesn't really matter anymore anyways, so I suppose it's no difference. You will remember me, after all.

You're going to arrive in a small seaside town. An object I need is stashed there, in one of the buildings - I can't recall where it was placed, but I would like you to retrieve it. Succeed, and your power will grow far beyond than you know. I need not remind you the price of failure.

I look forward to hearing your report, my ever-dutiful pawn.

Your Informant,
[[The Informant]]

# [[Realm of Knowledge]], [[The Room/The Circle/Town Kingdom/index|Town Kingdom]]
To my pawn,

It seems your journeys have taken you far. I heard your prayers in the midst of night, searching for the source with the answers to all your questions. Don't you remember that all calls for scholarly searches go through me?

It has been quite some time since I visited this library, but for you, the visit was worth it. I need something from this place.

In the mountain's core lies a red mineral. When ground into dust, it exhibits many useful properties that the people of this land have used for spices and medicinal remedies, though they miss out on its true beauty in doing so.

I would like you to gather a pouch's worth of this mineral. I would do it myself, but you have an eye for precious things. Try not to choose the wrong gem - you will certainly know it when you see it.

I wish you the best of luck Zephyr. Prepare for my presence shortly.

Cordially,
The Informant
