An older man, he appeared from thin air after the party defeated the [[Statue's Shadow|Statue's Shadow]] in the [[Haven of Sight]], temporarily delaying their descent into the mountain. Wrinkled skin, bones shaking, he somehow moves with a speed faster than even [[The Lady of the Forest]], gliding almost recklessly with intuition.

His own vision gone, he acts as a guide to [[The Room/The Pawns/index|The Pawns]] he comes across, making clear the tangled prophecies and memories they unravel.

He was the first individual to give the party relatively straight answers to the questions they asked him. It was revealed that he was [[Inbad|Inbad's]] master, instructor, and mentor in his [[The Kingdom|kingdom]], and that he is the embodiment of Sight, swiftness, and capability of this current realm.

Upon offering the complete answer to any one query, he divulged that the party was brought together to be pawns in a game of six, each one in the service of a [[The Room/The Players/index|Player]] with their own motives and will. With a blink, he disappeared, a debt fulfilled that left the party with more uncertainty than closure.
