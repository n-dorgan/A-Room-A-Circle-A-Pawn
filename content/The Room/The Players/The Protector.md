Seen journeying towards the mountains, the party met The Protector as she guarded a youthful [[Protector's Ward|blue dragon]] resting within a grassy field. Self-proclaimed Protector of [[The Plains]], she is a guarding force of this realm, the last line of defense against the encroaching [[The Room/The Shadows/index|Shadows]], of which the party warned her.

Her majestic scales shine in the sunlight, determined to aid the party when they will need it most, especially [[Jaime]], whom she knighted as a protector of his own right, carrying ancient ties to his [[House of Lancaster|family]] name. He felt this protection in a moment of near death, hanging at altitude so high the clouds covered the ground below; she blessed Jaime with [[Domain of Might|might]], holding true to her word and promising it will not be the last time she will intervene.
