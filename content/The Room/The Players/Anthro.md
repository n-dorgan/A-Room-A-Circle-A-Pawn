A frequent patron of [[The Room and Circle]], his name carries more meaning than one. He claims to [[The Room/The Pawns/index|The Pawns]], "I am you, all of you," a strange method for a man with a burning fire in his eyes, wishing to shape the world in his vision. Should the party need him, all they need to do  is call his name.

Though, it is unclear how he might assist, since he was impaled by a [[The Room/The Shadows/index|shadow]] while sending the party off from [[The Room/The Circle/Lubellum/index|Lubellum]] and is presumed dead, his promise rings true in your ears. Everyone you meet seems to have fond memories of the fiendish man who taunted you, recalling him as a good friend and person, capturing what it means to be human, a stark contrast to what you witnessed.

What did he endure to lead to him to fall so far?
