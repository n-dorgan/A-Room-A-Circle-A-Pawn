Keeper of [[The Room/The Deck/index|The Deck]], she has watched from [[The Room/The Shadows/index|The Shadows]], offering fragmented shards of information to the party. She always appears at the inopportune moment, seemingly bending time to perform readings of fate. Her presence has previously foretold that of [[The Informant]], who is always nearby, and never with pleasant news. Yet, the more you see her, the stranger her readings have become - perhaps a call to action, or a warning, for the coming days of  [[The Room/The Pawns/index|The Pawns]]...
